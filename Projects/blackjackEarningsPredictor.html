Blackjack & Casino Earnings Predictor

A Monte Carlo simulation tool that models blackjack wagering requirements, house rules, and betting strategies to evaluate the expected value (EV) of casino promotions.

Python Monte Carlo Simulation Multiprocessing NumPy Matplotlib

Overview

Many online casinos offer promotions like "50% deposit match up to $250", but these bonuses come with wagering requirements that must be met before withdrawing. Because games like blackjack often contribute only 10-20% toward these targets, a player must wager thousands of dollars to clear a small bonus.

I built the Casino Earnings Predictor to simulate millions of blackjack hands using Monte Carlo methods to help players determine whether a promotion is mathematically positive expected value (+EV) or a statistical trap.

Key Simulation Modules

  • Playthrough Simulator (simulate_playthrough.py): Models sessions targeting a specific wager threshold to calculate risk of ruin (bust rate), average hands needed to clear, and hourly win rate.
  • Bankroll Simulator (simulate_bankroll.py): Compares betting strategies (e.g., withdrawing immediately vs. churning credits) to visualize bankroll variance.

Technical Highlights

⚡ Parallel Engine

Utilizes Python's multiprocessing library to distribute simulation sessions across all available CPU cores. This allows the simulator to model over 100,000 gaming sessions (millions of individual hands) in seconds.

⚙️ Rule Customization

Features a custom blackjack rule engine configuring deck count, split rules, double-down options, late surrender, and dealer behavior (hitting or standing on soft 17) to match exact online casino rules.

📈 Visual Analytics

Generates detailed visual dashboards using NumPy and Matplotlib, plotting standard deviation curves, bankroll progression paths, and expected profit distributions to illustrate risk vs. reward.
